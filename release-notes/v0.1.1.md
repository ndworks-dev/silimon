Silimon now has a settings window and can open when you sign in.

## What's new

- **Settings window:** choose the menu bar metrics, the refresh interval and check for updates in one place. Open it with *Settings…* in the menu.
- **Open at Login:** start Silimon when you sign in. It stays off until you turn it on.
- **A simpler menu:** the dropdown now holds the readings, with an update option that appears only when a new version is available.

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://ndworks.dev/silimon/install.sh | sh
```

Or download [`Silimon.dmg`](https://ndworks.dev/silimon/download) and drag the app to Applications. Silimon 0.1.0 offers this update on its own.

**Requires** an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later.
