Silimon now has a way to support its development.

## What's new

- **Support on Ko-fi:** if Silimon is useful to you, the new *Support* section at the end of Settings opens Silimon's Ko-fi page in your browser.

## Install

```sh
curl -fsSL https://ndworks.dev/silimon/install.sh | sh
```

Or download [`Silimon.dmg`](https://ndworks.dev/silimon/download) and drag the app to Applications. Silimon 0.1.0 and 0.1.1 offer this update on their own.

**Requires** an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later.
